# 🇨🇳 Hangzhou

> **Why visit:** West Lake scenery, Jiangnan history, Lingyin Temple, Hangzhou's waterways, and local favorites such as xiaolongbao, pian'erchuan noodles, and Hangzhou-style cuisine. This guide focuses on a **two-day itinerary**, with alternatives for a one-day trip and families. Some opening hours, transit schedules, restaurant reviews, and prices come from trip-planning notes; reconfirm them before traveling.

## 📍 Overview
- **First-time highlights:** Prioritize **West Lake + Lingyin Temple/Feilai Peak + Water Bus Line 7 and the Southern Song old town** for a combination of scenery, Buddhist heritage, waterways, and Southern Song history.
- **Recommended duration:** Two days. If you have only one, focus on West Lake rather than trying to squeeze Lingyin, the Grand Canal, and Jiuxi into the same day.
- **Pacing:** Spend Day 1 around West Lake. On Day 2, take an early boat along the East River, explore the old town in the morning, and visit Lingyin Temple and Maojiabu in the afternoon.
- **Summer:** Hangzhou is hot and humid, with plenty of outdoor walking. Bring drinking water, sun protection, and insect repellent; skip Baoshi Hill in thunderstorms.
- **Where to stay:** Hubin/Longxiangqiao, Fengqi Road/Kerry Centre, and Wulin Square are convenient. The itinerary below uses **Midtown Shangri-La, Hangzhou** as its hotel reference point.

**Timing and risk:** Day 2 is packed, especially given the Line 7 queue, Deshou Palace admission, afternoon Lingyin Temple reservation, and evening train. **Don't jeopardize your temple entry or onward travel by waiting too long for the boat or a particular snack stall.** If the morning boat queue exceeds about 30–45 minutes, use metro or a ride-hailing service to reach Meihuabei or Deshou Palace instead. If delayed in the afternoon, shorten or drop Wangxian Pavilion, the Maojiabu walk, and then the second visit to Broken Bridge. Reserve **Lingyin's afternoon slot**.

**Seasonal Line 7 note:** An announcement for July 2026 listed split service periods such as **07:40–10:20 and 15:40–17:00** at Baziqiao. These are historical hours, **not a standing timetable**. Check the latest Hangzhou Water Bus notices or call **0571-88024168**. An October 2026 report also mentioned long queues at popular piers, supporting an early start.

# 🌊 West Lake

## 🏞️ Essential Sights and Route
- **Three Pools Mirroring the Moon (Santanyinyue):** One of the Ten Scenes of West Lake, pictured on the back of the Chinese 1-yuan banknote. You typically take a boat to the island. **Board near Hubin and leave toward Flower Harbor** if available; confirm pier and exit routes on the day.
- **Viewing Fish at Flower Harbor (Huagang Guanyu):** Red carp pond, greenery, and waterside scenery; particularly nice for families. Respect park rules on feeding wildlife.
- **Spring Dawn at Su Causeway:** A roughly 2.8-km causeway built using dredged material when Su Shi oversaw work on West Lake. Allow more than an hour to walk its length, and longer with photo stops.
- **Lotus in the Breeze at Crooked Courtyard (Quyuan Fenghe):** A prime spot for summer lotus blossoms; combine with the northern part of Su Causeway.
- **Solitary Hill, Autumn Moon over the Calm Lake, and Bai Causeway:** A scenic and cultural walk from Xiling Bridge to Broken Bridge.
- **Broken Bridge in Snow (Duanqiao Canxue):** A signature landmark; in summer enjoy the bridge and lake views rather than expecting snow.
- **Baoshi Hill / Baochu Pagoda:** Elevated views of the lake, Bai Causeway, and skyline. Watch for slippery paths, thunderstorms, and safe descent after sunset.
- **Leifeng Pagoda / Jingci Temple:** If skipping Baoshi Hill, consider these southern-lake landmarks for an easier alternative.

## 🚣 Boats, Walking, and Cycling with Kids
- **West Lake boats:** Ask at lakeside piers such as First Park or Second Park whether you can visit **Three Pools Mirroring the Moon** and disembark toward **Flower Harbor**; some services require another queue or transfer.
- **Walking:** Su Causeway, Bai Causeway, and Solitary Hill are ideal on foot. Avoid extended unshaded walking at midday in summer.
- **Family cycling:** Some local public bikes (“little red bikes”) may have child seats. Check availability, return stations, pricing, and child-rider rules through **Dingda Chuxing** or an official service; not all lakefront paths permit cycling.
- **West Lake musical fountain:** **19:30** is a reference time only. Check the latest official schedule and whether performances are operating.

# 🛕 Lingyin Temple & Feilai Peak
- **Highlights:** Buddhist stone carvings at Feilai Peak, Lingyin Temple's halls, and religious history. A fuller visit generally takes **2.5–3 hours**.
- **Day 2 afternoon route:** Feilai Peak sculptures → Lingyin Temple's main halls (including the Mahavira Hall and Medicine Buddha Hall) → exit toward the southeast gate following on-site instructions. Yongfu Temple is an optional extension.
- **Reservations:** Under the **2026 policy described in the trip notes**, Lingyin–Feilai Peak offered free admission with **real-name timed reservations**, roughly **07:30–12:00** for the morning and **12:00–17:00** for the afternoon. Use the official **“杭州灵隐飞来峰”** entry in WeChat or Alipay to check current policy, availability, and hours rather than relying on old travel guides.
- **Practical tip:** Take precautions against summer heat and confirm the exits, bus stops, and best route to Maojiabu beforehand.
- **After your visit on Day 2:** Take a bus from the Lingyin Temple area to Maojiabu for a short stroll, then another bus toward northern West Lake. Skip the repeat visit to Broken Bridge if short on time.
- **Official reference:** [Hangzhou West Lake Scenic Area Administration](https://westlake.hangzhou.gov.cn/).

# 🚤 Grand Canal, Water Buses & Old Streets

## 🛶 Water Bus Routes
- **Line 7 (Day 2 early-morning priority):** Plan to board at the **Baziqiao** starting pier, passing Baoshan Bridge, Guang'an New Bridge, Chaoming Temple Lane, Caishi Bridge, Kuixiang, Zhejiang No. 2 Hospital, and Chengzhan Railway Station before reaching **Meihuabei**. This connects well to Deshou Palace, Hu Xueyan's Former Residence, and the Southern Song old streets. The trip notes give a reference fare of around **3 yuan**, but routes, departures, capacity, and payment options can change.
- **Queue early:** Some Line 7 boats have very limited seating; waits can be long during holidays and busy periods. Arrive before the first departure when possible. Heat, water levels, and maintenance can interrupt service. Check official **Hangzhou Water Bus** updates.
- **Line 1:** Runs via areas such as Wulinmen, Xinyifang, and Gongchen Bridge; better for a dedicated trip along the main Grand Canal corridor.
- **Backup:** If the wait exceeds about 30–45 minutes, use the metro or ride-hailing to reach Deshou Palace so the afternoon Lingyin visit remains feasible.

## 🏯 Southern Song Old Town: Meihuabei to Zhongshan South Road
- **Southern Song Deshou Palace Ruins Museum:** Archaeological remains of a Southern Song palace and related exhibitions. Prioritize this in the morning; check current reservations and opening hours.
- **Hu Xueyan's Former Residence:** Learn about a prominent late-Qing merchant and traditional Hangzhou mansion architecture. Convenient to visit after Deshou Palace.
- **Wangxian Pavilion:** At **468 Zhongshan South Road**, near the Drum Tower. Offers views toward Hu Xueyan's residence and Wushan, along with the archaeological remains of Southern Song workshops below. Public tourism material has listed **Tuesday–Sunday, 09:30–12:00 and 13:30–16:30** as reference hours; confirm locally.
- **Drum Tower / Southern Song Imperial Street / Southern Song Post Office:** Photo stops and historical streets. If the themed post office is closed, the rest of the route still works.
- **Lunch on Zhongshan South Road and Grandma Zhao's Cong Bao Hui:** Wander the traditional neighborhood, then look for the local cong bao hui snack stall.

## 🏘️ Other Grand Canal Attractions
- **Gongchen Bridge:** Historic bridge representing Hangzhou's Grand Canal shipping heritage.
- **Qiaoxi Historic District:** Traditional lanes, handicrafts, and industrial history along the canal.
- **Grand Canal Museum and the Knife, Scissors & Sword / Umbrella / Fan Museums:** Allow a separate half-day and check museum closure days.
- **Xiaohe Straight Street and Dadou Road:** Good for canal walks, tea, and everyday neighborhood atmosphere.

# 🍜 Hangzhou Food

## 🥟 Traditional Snacks & Breakfast
- **Shrimp-and-pork xiaolongbao, wontons, and beef vermicelli soup:** **Xinfeng Snacks (新丰小吃)**, a practical breakfast or quick lunch option. The notes also mention shrimp wontons and yangtanggao (洋糖糕).
- **Scallion pan-fried buns:** **Zhao Baomei Dim Sum (赵宝美点心)**, at **108 Chengtou Lane** in the notes. Try another time; Day 2 lunch focuses on old-town snacks along Zhongshan South Road.
- **Savory soy milk and fried dough sticks:** **Youbu Soy Milk (游埠豆浆)**, better when you are not rushing.
- **Cong Bao Hui (葱包桧):** A Hangzhou snack made by wrapping fried dough and scallions in a thin pancake and pressing it on a griddle.
- **Shaomai and fresh-pork-and-pickled-mustard mooncakes:** Look for **Jingfang Zhou Dajie (景芳周大姐)** and **Liuxianji (六贤记)**.

## 🍜 Hangzhou Noodles
- **Pian'erchuan (片儿川):** Classic noodles with pickled mustard greens, bamboo shoots, and sliced meat.
- **Shrimp-and-eel noodles (虾爆鳝面):** Shrimp and eel with noodles. **Banchuan (拌川)** and kidney banchuan are also worth trying.
- **Places to consider:** **Kuiyuan Guan (奎元馆)**, **Juying Noodle Shop (菊英面馆)**, **Xiaogou Noodle Shop (小狗面馆)**, and **Fang Laoda Noodles (方老大面)**. Choose a convenient location along your route.

## 🍖 Hangzhou-Style Cuisine & Sit-Down Meals
- **Typical dishes:** Dongpo pork, Longjing shrimp, stir-fried shrimp, crab-roe tofu, braised tofu skin, and other Jiangnan home-style dishes.
- **Restaurants to consider:** **Yema (叶马)** near Kerry Centre, **Jinzhu Restaurant (金猪餐厅)**, **Xinxin Hotel 1913 (新新饭店1913)**, **Deming Restaurant (德明饭店)**, and **Hang San Jie Mei (杭三姐妹)**. Verify opening times and menus.
- **Zhi Wei Guan (知味观):** Known for snacks and Dongpo pork; some travel reviews favored its pork over the xiaolongbao at certain branches.
- **Xin Bailu (新白鹿), Grandma's Home (外婆家), and Green Tea Restaurant (绿茶):** Convenient alternatives with approachable prices, but not necessarily worth a long queue.
- **Crab-roe noodles:** **Li Bai Xie (李百蟹蟹黄面)** and **Xie Ji Wang (蟹记王)** are possibilities, usually pricier than ordinary noodles.
- **Night markets and food streets:** **Wulin Night Market**, **Shengli River Food Street**, and **Dadou Road** work well with an evening stroll.

# 🏨 Accommodation & Transportation

## 🛏️ Where to Stay
- **Hubin / Longxiangqiao:** Convenient for first-time travelers focusing on West Lake.
- **Kerry Centre / Fengqi Road / Wulin Square:** Plenty of dining and shopping, with useful connections to other neighborhoods.
- **Midtown Shangri-La, Hangzhou (FHR):** Close to Kerry Centre. If booked through a hotel-benefits program, confirm the breakfast guest count, dining credit, room upgrades, and other conditions with the hotel and your booking terms; don't assume someone else's experience applies.

## 🚇 Practical Transit Notes
- **Bus + metro + ride-hailing:** On Day 2, travel to Baziqiao pier early, then from the Southern Song old town to Lingyin Temple in the afternoon. Use live route guidance for Lingyin → Maojiabu → West Lake.
- **Water Bus Line 7:** A priority experience on the second morning, but queues, capacity limits, and cancellations are unpredictable. Keep a road-based backup.
- **Hangzhou East Railway Station:** If your train leaves at **20:00**, leave enough time to retrieve luggage, travel to the station, and clear security. Don't plan sightseeing until the last minute.
- **Summer storms and heat:** Hiking, boating, and outdoor activities may be affected; keep indoor museums and food stops as alternatives.
- **Rail tickets:** [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/).

## 🗓️ If You Only Have One Day
- **Relaxed route:** Hubin → Three Pools Mirroring the Moon → Flower Harbor → a section of Su Causeway → Leifeng Pagoda **or** Jingci Temple → evening by the lake.
- **Walking/photography route:** Bai Causeway → Broken Bridge → Solitary Hill → Crooked Courtyard → Su Causeway, with an optional Baoshi Hill climb around sunset if weather permits.
- **History and culture route:** Lingyin Temple/Feilai Peak in the morning, key West Lake sights in the afternoon; **do not also add Gongchen Bridge**.

## 🗺️ Worth a Longer Stay
- **Jiuxi, Longjing, Shili Langdang, and Yunqi Bamboo Path:** Tea hills and hiking trails, better with another half-day or full day.
- **Maojiabu and Yuhu Bay:** Quieter wetland and lakeside scenery away from the busiest waterfront.
- **Wuliu Lane:** An extension near Deshou Palace; the Day 2 route already includes Deshou Palace and Hu Xueyan's Former Residence, so no need to add another detour.
- **Xixi Wetland:** Best planned as a longer separate visit, not squeezed into this two-day itinerary.
