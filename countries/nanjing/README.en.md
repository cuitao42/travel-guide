# 🇨🇳 Nanjing (南京)

> **Why visit:** Nanjing preserves important landmarks from its history as a capital of several dynasties, the Ming dynasty, and modern China. Visitors can explore the Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre by Japanese Invaders, the Presidential Palace, Nanjing Museum, the Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum, and the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, then enjoy the Qinhuai River at night. This guide offers a **flexible two-day suggested itinerary**.

## 📍 Overview
- **Suggested length of stay:** **2–3 days** for a first visit. Two days can cover the major sights, but require early starts and efficient use of the metro and scenic-area shuttles.
- **Two themes:** Day 1 focuses on modern history and Qinhuai culture; Day 2 explores Ming-era and Republican-era landmarks in the Zhongshan Scenic Area.
- **Where to stay:** **Daxinggong, Xinjiekou, or the Confucius Temple area** offer convenient transport. Staying near Nanjing South Railway Station can be useful for early or late trains.
- **Seasons:** Spring and autumn are pleasant for walking. **Nanjing summers are hot and humid**, and the Zhongshan Scenic Area has many outdoor paths and steps. Carry water and sun protection. Bring rain gear during the plum-rain season; winters can be damp and cold, especially on the hills.
- **Reservations to prioritize:** The **Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall, Nanjing Museum, and Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum**. Check Monday closures and special holiday arrangements separately.

# 🏛️ Historical and Downtown Attractions

## 🕯️ Memorial Hall of the Victims in Nanjing Massacre by Japanese Invaders (侵华日军南京大屠杀遇难同胞纪念馆)
- **Significance:** Documents the Nanjing Massacre and the experiences of victims and survivors. It is an important place for understanding the history of China's resistance against Japanese aggression.
- **Visiting time:** Allow **at least two hours** and follow the exhibition route. This is a place of remembrance, not an ordinary photo stop.
- **Etiquette:** Remain quiet and respectful. **Photography is prohibited or restricted in areas such as the remains exhibition hall and the “Mass Grave of Ten Thousand Corpses”; follow posted rules.**
- **Getting there:** Near **Yunjin Road Station, Metro Line 2**. Check the exact entrance in your reservation information.
- **Reservations:** Admission is generally free with real-name reservations. Check release dates, closures, and entry times on the [official reservation platform](https://exhibit.19371213.com.cn/yuyue/).

## 🏛️ Presidential Palace (南京总统府)
- **Suggested route:** **Central section → western section → eastern section**.
  - **Central section:** Main hall, the “The World Belongs to All” plaque, reception rooms, and Zichao Building.
  - **Western section:** Xuyuan Garden, Sun Yat-sen's Provisional Presidential Office, and related historical buildings.
  - **Eastern section:** Former Executive Yuan buildings, East Garden, and other sites depending on available time.
- **Visiting time:** About **1.5–2.5 hours**, or longer for visitors interested in the Republican period and architecture.
- **Getting there:** Walk from **Daxinggong Metro Station**.
- **Before visiting:** Check reservations, ticket prices, last admission, and Monday arrangements at the [official Presidential Palace website](https://www.njztf.cn/opening_hours.html).

## 🏺 Nanjing Museum (南京博物院)
- **Name clarification:** The institution is **Nanjing Museum (南京博物院)**; when searching or booking, use the exact Chinese name rather than the generic term 南京博物馆.
- **Highlights:** History Gallery, Republic of China Gallery, Art Gallery, Special Exhibition Gallery, and extensive collections illustrating Jiangsu and Chinese history.
- **Visiting time:** Allow **at least 2.5–3 hours**; a half day or more is worthwhile if you enjoy museums.
- **Getting there:** Near **Minggugong Station, Metro Line 2**.
- **Reservations:** Verify reservation-release times, ID requirements, and closures on the [Nanjing Museum website](https://www.njmuseum.org.cn/).

## 🌃 Confucius Temple, Jiangnan Examination Hall, and Qinhuai River
- **Confucius Temple (夫子庙):** A lively traditional-style shopping area, especially after dark. It is highly commercialized, so focus on its history and riverside atmosphere.
- **Jiangnan Examination Hall / China Imperial Examination Museum (江南贡院 / 中国科举博物馆):** An important site for learning about China's imperial examination system, especially interesting for students. Allow **1–1.5 hours** and verify evening opening hours.
- **Qinhuai River (秦淮河):** Walk along the river at dusk or after dark. **Wende Bridge (文德桥)** and nearby spots offer views of illuminated buildings and reflections.
- **Laomendong (老门东):** Historic-style lanes, snacks, and shops; an optional extension rather than a required stop.
- **Suggested route:** **Confucius Temple → Imperial Examination Museum → Dengke Corridor → Wende Bridge → Qinhuai River → Laomendong (optional)**.
- **Food tip:** You do not need to eat a full meal in the busiest part of the Confucius Temple district. Nearby residential streets and Kexiang also offer plenty of choices.
- [Imperial Examination Museum visitor information](https://www.njiemuseum.com/canguan/1.html)

# 🌳 Zhongshan Scenic Area / Purple Mountain (钟山风景区 / 紫金山)

## 🐉 Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum: Zhu Yuanzhang and the Ming Dynasty
- **History:** The Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum is the tomb of **Zhu Yuanzhang**, the first Ming emperor, and Empress Ma, and a major example of Ming imperial funerary architecture.
- **Main sights:** **Stone Elephant Road → Sacred Way → Golden Water Bridge → mausoleum complex / Fangcheng and Minglou**. Adapt the walking order to the entrance used.
- **What to see:** Stone animal and human statues, wooded historical paths, and imperial tomb architecture. Plum Blossom Hill is particularly attractive in spring, while autumn foliage is another highlight.
- **Visiting time:** Typically **2–3 hours**, or longer for a thorough visit.
- **Entrance and transfer:** **Gate 1** is recommended in the source notes to reduce walking. Consider the scenic-area electric shuttle to Meiling Palace or the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum.

## 🏠 Meiling Palace (美龄宫, Optional)
- A villa and exhibition site associated with the Republican era; convenient to visit between Ming Xiaoling and the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum.
- Skip it when short on time, in extreme heat, or if your group is tired, and spend longer at the main mausoleum sites instead.

## 🏛️ Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (中山陵)
- **Main approach:** **Bo'ai Archway → “The World Belongs to All” gate → stele pavilion → long staircase → memorial hall**.
- **Reservations:** Entry to the central mausoleum area is generally **free but requires a real-name reservation**. Check announcements from the [Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum Administration](https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/).
- **Visiting time:** Allow **1–1.5 hours**. The many steps and direct sunlight can be tiring in summer; carry water and maintain a respectful atmosphere in the memorial hall.

## 🎼 Music Stage (音乐台)
- A semi-circular outdoor stage and lawn near the mausoleum, known for its historic performance setting and flocks of pigeons.
- A good short stop, although it can be crowded. Decide whether to enter based on your interests and schedule.
- Consider taking the scenic-area electric shuttle onward to Linggu Temple.

## 🌲 Linggu Temple and Linggu Scenic Area (灵谷寺 / 灵谷景区)
- **Highlights:** Linggu Temple, Linggu Pagoda, and shaded forest paths. The pagoda can offer elevated views, subject to access and opening conditions.
- **Itinerary:** The final stop on the Zhongshan route. If everyone is tired, it is fine to finish after the Music Stage instead.
- **Leaving:** At the **Linggu Scenic Area exit**, check bus stops for connections to a convenient metro station. Older guides mention **Bus 202**, but use real-time navigation rather than relying on that number.
- **Opening hours:** The temple, scenic area, and pagoda may have different last-entry and access rules; do not assume everything will remain open until evening.

## 🎟️ Tickets and Transportation Within Zhongshan
- Reservations for the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum and admission to Ming Xiaoling, Meiling Palace, the Music Stage, and Linggu are **managed separately**. Multi-attraction passes may be offered.
- A combination ticket of around **CNY 100**, a Music Stage ticket of around **CNY 10**, and Meiling Palace admission of around **CNY 30** are **historical references only**. Check official prices before purchasing.
- The scenic area is extensive. Different shuttle routes may require different tickets; check the route map rather than assuming one ticket permits unlimited travel.
- [Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum Administration visitor information](https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202506/t20250623_5591074.html)

# 🥟 Nanjing Food Guide

## 🦆 Local Specialties Worth Trying
- **Salted duck / preserved duck (盐水鸭 / 板鸭):** Signature Nanjing duck dishes. Local delicatessens in residential neighborhoods can be good choices; check freshness, prices, and customer traffic.
- **Duck blood vermicelli soup (鸭血粉丝汤):** Commonly made with duck broth, duck blood, vermicelli, and duck offal; suitable for breakfast or a light meal.
- **Soup dumplings / xiaolongbao (汤包 / 小笼包):** Be careful of the hot broth inside. They pair well with duck blood vermicelli soup.
- **Beef potstickers (牛肉锅贴):** Juicy filling and a crisp pan-fried base; a classic Nanjing breakfast snack.
- **Pidumian, noodles with puffed pork skin (皮肚面):** A Nanjing noodle specialty with a variety of toppings.
- **Rice cakes and sweet rice-ball desserts (糕团 / 小元宵):** Snacks to try around the Qinhuai River or traditional streets.

## 🍴 Places to Consider
- **Duck delicatessens:** Zhangyun Banya (章云板鸭), Jinhongxing Duck Shop (金宏兴鸭子店), and similar shops.
- **Duck blood vermicelli soup:** Xiaopanji (小潘记), Yadebao (鸭得堡), and Laoshengqing (老盛庆).
- **Beef potstickers:** Liji Halal Restaurant (李记清真馆), among others.
- **Food areas:** **Kexiang (科巷), Sanqibaxiang (三七八巷), and Laomendong (老门东)**. Check recent opening information, menus, and prices before visiting.

**Meals along the two-day route:** After visiting the Memorial Hall on Day 1, head toward Daxinggong for **soup dumplings or xiaolongbao with duck blood vermicelli soup**; you could also buy some salted duck. Try snacks near the Qinhuai River in the evening. On Day 2, prioritize a convenient lunch, rest, and hydration in the Zhongshan Scenic Area rather than detouring for a particular restaurant.

# 🚇 Transportation, Accommodation, and Seasonal Tips

## 🚉 Getting Around
- **Metro:** A practical way to reach key downtown attractions and Nanjing South Railway Station while avoiding road congestion.
- **Useful stations:** **Yunjin Road** for the Memorial Hall, **Daxinggong** for the Presidential Palace, **Minggugong** for Nanjing Museum, and **Fuzimiao (Confucius Temple)** for the Confucius Temple district.
- **Zhongshan Scenic Area:** Start near an entrance to Ming Xiaoling and combine walking with sightseeing shuttles. After visiting Linggu, connect by bus to the metro. Check routes and last departure times on Amap (Gaode Maps) or Baidu Maps.
- **Nanjing South Railway Station:** On train-departure days, allow time for metro travel, security checks, and walking within the station. Avoid scheduling a major attraction during the hour immediately before departure.

## ☀️ Weather and Crowds
- **Summer:** Hot and humid. Visit outdoor sites such as Ming Xiaoling and the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum early; use midday for indoor attractions or meals. Bring sun protection, water, and electrolytes as needed.
- **Plum rains and thunderstorms:** Carry light rain gear and shorten forest or hillside walks when paths are slippery.
- **Holidays and weekends:** Nanjing Museum, the Memorial Hall, the Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum, and Confucius Temple can be crowded. Reserve ahead and allow extra transport time.
- **Autumn:** The forests of Zhongshan and the plane-tree-lined avenues are especially scenic, making it a better season for long walks than midsummer.

## 🏨 Where to Stay
- **Daxinggong (大行宫):** Convenient for the Presidential Palace, Nanjing Museum, and Confucius Temple; a strong choice for first-time visitors.
- **Xinjiekou (新街口):** Plenty of shopping and dining, with good metro access.
- **Confucius Temple / Qinhuai River:** Convenient for evening walks, but busy areas may have higher hotel prices.
- **Nanjing South Railway Station:** Useful for very early or late trains, though farther from some sights.

## 🗺️ If You Have a Third Day
- **Nanjing City Wall (Jiefang Gate / Taicheng section):** Elevated views of Xuanwu Lake, Purple Mountain, and the city.
- **Xuanwu Lake + Jiming Temple:** A pleasant half-day walk, with the Nanjing Paleontology Museum nearby.
- **Niushou Mountain (牛首山):** Farther from the city center; best given half a day or more.
- **Pioneer Bookstore, Six Dynasties Museum, and areas around Nanjing University or Southeast University:** Choose according to your interests; verify campus access rules.
- **Zhonghua Gate Fortress, Laomendong, and other sections of the city wall:** Ideal for visitors interested in fortifications and leisurely exploration.

## ⚠️ Reservations and Information Currency
- **Reserve the Memorial Hall, Nanjing Museum, and Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum first**, then finalize your itinerary. Venues may close on Mondays or change arrangements for memorial dates and holidays.
- Ticket prices, bus routes, and opening hours in this guide may change and should not be treated as real-time information.
- If you enjoy exploring museums in depth, spread this two-day route over **three days** rather than rushing important exhibitions.
